# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Windows Codex users who need a passive, glanceable view of their live usage allowances while they work. The primary user keeps the app running all day and should not need to open Codex settings or calculate reset countdowns manually.

## Product Purpose

Codex Limit Reminder reads every rate-limit window exposed by the user's signed-in local Codex installation, preserves the latest state, and makes usage, remaining capacity, and reset timing visible before work is interrupted. Success means the information stays current automatically, is readable in seconds, and never adds setup beyond choosing a daily alert time.

## Brand Personality

Discreet, precise, futuristic. The app should feel like a dependable Windows instrument: quiet during normal work, direct when capacity becomes scarce, and technical without decorative visual noise.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a full analytics dashboard, a large permanent desktop panel, a gaming overlay, or a decorative glassmorphism demo. Avoid dense charts, oversized controls, gratuitous motion, and attention-seeking color when no limit is at risk.

## Design Principles

1. Make the current weekly capacity and reset understandable at a glance.
2. Reveal detail progressively instead of keeping a dashboard open.
3. Stay out of the user's way without hiding important risk.
4. Reflect exactly what Codex reports; never imply a fixed calendar cycle.
5. Preserve user control through clear keyboard, tray, expand, collapse, and hide paths.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use Windows system colors and high-contrast behavior, keyboard-accessible controls, readable text at common DPI settings, and status labels that do not rely on color alone. Keep transitions instant or subtle so reduced-motion users lose no information.
